About

The most hassle-free way to download videos. Any site, any service, anytime.

Queenfisher is a one-click multimedia converter and downloader powered by yt-dlp, the open source downloader that works with thousands of sites and gets fixed fast whenever one of them changes. Download any and all media without suspicious external sites or p2p vulnerabilities. Simple, sleek, and safe to use.

Formats:
MP4, MKV, WebM, MOV, AVI, FLV and GIF, or MP3, WAV, FLAC, OGG, M4A, ALAC and the original sound.
Extras:
subtitles, whole playlists, and skipping sponsor segments.
Updates:
yt-dlp keeps itself current, checked against its developers' signature. When a site changes, Queenfisher updates it and tries again.
Privacy:
no ads, no accounts, no tracking. Once a day it asks GitHub for the newest version number, and Settings can turn that off.
Runs on:
Windows 10/11, macOS, Linux
Written in:
C++, Qt 6
License:
GPLv3, free and open source

Features

Which format should I pick?
MP4:
plays everywhere. Up to 1080p on YouTube.
MKV, WebM:
the best quality the site has, 1440p and 4K included.
MOV:
for video editors and Apple devices.
AVI, FLV:
for old players. AVI is converted, which takes a while.
GIF:
short clips up to a minute, no sound.
MP3, M4A:
music for any player or phone.
WAV, FLAC, ALAC:
for editing or music libraries. Bigger files, same sound.
Original:
the site's own sound as it is, nothing converted.
Subtitles, playlists and sponsors
Subtitles:
English subtitles inside the video, when the site has them (MP4, MKV, WebM).
Whole playlist:
a link from a playlist downloads every video in it.
Skip sponsors:
cuts sponsored segments out of YouTube videos, using the SponsorBlock community's list.
Where downloads go

Your Downloads folder. Change... next to "Save to" picks another one, and Queenfisher remembers it, along with your format, quality and extras.

Downloading the same video again

Another quality is saved as its own file. The same choice again asks whether to keep the file you have or overwrite it; the old file stays until the new one is complete.

The Library

Every finished download, newest first. Search it, open a file, or show it in its folder. Files you delete or move disappear from it by themselves.

Keeping it running in the tray

Minimizing (or View, Minimize to Tray) hides Queenfisher in the system tray while downloads carry on. Click the fish to bring it back.

Troubleshooting

Windows says it protected my PC

Windows warns about new programs it hasn't seen often. Click More info, then Run anyway. Every release is checked on VirusTotal before it's published.

macOS says it can't be opened

Click Done, then open System Settings, Privacy & Security, and click Open Anyway near the bottom. macOS asks only the first time.

It says ffmpeg is missing (macOS, Linux)

Install it once: brew install ffmpeg on macOS, or from your package manager on Linux. On Windows Queenfisher fetches its own.

A download failed
  • The Status column says what happened; Log, under the downloads, shows the details.
  • Select it and click Retry. When a site has changed, Queenfisher updates yt-dlp by itself first.
YouTube asks me to sign in

Queenfisher tries once more with your default browser's sign-in, so sign in to YouTube there. On Windows, Chrome and Edge often lock their sign-in away from other programs; a Firefox-based browser works.

YouTube only offers low qualities

YouTube needs Deno for its better qualities. Queenfisher fetches it by itself; if that failed (it says so when it starts), check your connection and restart Queenfisher.

Still stuck?

Open an issue on GitHub with what you tried and what the Log says.
