yt-dlp wrapper

About

IN DEV

A small desktop app for yt-dlp: paste a link, pick Audio or Video, click Download.

  • Fetches the official yt-dlp itself and checks its GPG signature and SHA-256
  • Keeps yt-dlp up to date, checking at most once a day
  • Audio keeps the site's original stream, with no re-encoding
  • No accounts, no telemetry, no extra services: everything goes through yt-dlp
Status:
Early development, nothing released yet
Runs on:
Windows, macOS, Linux (planned)
Built with:
Rust, Tauri
License:
MIT
Working name. Downloads come with the first release.
